Colors below are now read out of RVZ 06 itself — 14 pages, Affinity 3.2.3, art still live vector, so I pulled the fill values straight off the drawing operations rather than sampling pixels. Two limits of this export: it went out through PDFlib, which converts spot swatches to RGB, so I can read the exact RGB the file uses but not the Pantone ink builds behind them; and the type is outlined, so the fonts can't be read as font resources — the typography section still rests on glyph matching.

00

The mark

Desgastada lockup, as released — cut from Quiero Que Vengas cover art
Construction

Three fixed parts: LOS set small and tight, the crown in gold sitting on the apex of the V, and REVÉZ oversized underneath with the R reversed (Я) and the acute accent drawn as a solid slanted slab. ® sits at cap-height on the right. The crown's baseline arc and the V share a single vertical axis — the crown is not a free-floating element.

Two observed states

Desgastada — Ink Black #1B1919 with the worn speckle erosion, over the bone paper ground on both single covers. Solid knockout — Paper White #F3F4F2, no distress, over photography. The crown holds Brass Gold #A89360 in both; those three values are exactly what the source file uses.

The distress is not in the source export. The four Desgastada pages of RVZ 06 draw 15–21 vector paths each and embed zero images — enough for a clean lockup, nowhere near enough for the speckle erosion, and no raster for it to hide in. So the worn treatment is either a live Affinity effect that this flattened export dropped, or it is applied downstream in the cover art. Either way there is no scalable master of the distressed mark in what I have: reproducing it large or small needs the layered original.

01

Color

Six colors are used across the 14 pages of RVZ 06, and only six. The three below carry the mark; the rest are page grounds. Counts are how many times each is set as a fill or stroke in the file.

Ink Black
From source
#1B1919 · rgb(27, 25, 25) · 15 uses

The wordmark black, exact from the file — a warm off-black, not #000 and not the #0D0D0D the homepage draft assumed. Everything on this page sits on it.

Brass Gold
From source
#A89360 · rgb(168, 147, 96) · 23 uses

The most-used color in the file and the only chromatic one. Flag: this is the RGB the export wrote, not the ink — PANTONE 871 C is a metallic, its published sRGB approximation is the much darker #84754E, and the covers print it darker still (#87764C sampled). Treat #A89360 as the screen value and always send the spot to print. Where it needs to survive as a real metallic, that's a foil or an ink, never a hex.

Deck darks
From source
#222020 · 5 uses  /  #14151A · 2 uses

Two secondary darks in the later pages — one a hair lighter than the ink, one cooler. They read as presentation grounds rather than brand colors, so I haven't promoted them. Confirm if either is meant to be a surface color in its own right.

Bone
Sampled
#D2CEC3 · rgb(210, 206, 195)

Not in the logo file — this is the aged paper ground both single covers are built on, averaged across their untextured areas. It behaves as the brand's light surface in release art, but it is a cover-art decision rather than a value the mark specifies.

Paper White
From source
#F3F4F2 · rgb(243, 244, 242) · 20 uses

The knockout white of the mark — a fraction green, effectively neutral, and deliberately not the pure #FFFFFF the file also uses (9 times) for page grounds. Use #F3F4F2 for the mark and for text on dark; keep pure white for backgrounds only.

Red
Unresolved
#C0272D · 2.98:1 ON INK · 3.74:1 ON BONE

The draft's accent, carried forward under its own name. It appears nowhere in RVZ 06 or the release art, so #C0272D is a stand-in until an authoritative build arrives. Read the ratios as a hard limit: red is an accent — a rule, a tag, a fill behind white — and never body text on either ground.

Draft drift
Unresolved
#B8972A

The draft's gold — a more saturated, yellower guess at the brand gold. It should move to #A89360. The red that sat beside it here has been reclassified as Red and now has its own entry.

Default pairing
Ink Black ground, Paper White type, one Brass Gold accent per view. Gold is a highlight — eyebrows, rules, the single button. It does not carry body copy.
Inverted pairing
Bone ground, Ink Black type, Brass Gold rules and secondary titles — the exact build of both single covers.
Contrast check
Computed on the source values: Paper White on Ink Black is 15.9:1, Brass Gold on Ink Black is 5.8:1 — that clears AA at any size, so gold text and gold buttons are safe on dark. The reverse is not: gold on Bone is 1.9:1, unreadable at any size, and gold on pure white is 3.00:1 — exactly the large-text and UI threshold, so display sizes scrape a pass while body text fails. On light grounds treat the gold as art; anything that has to be read goes Ink Black.
02

Typography

The type in RVZ 06 is outlined — no font resources survive in the file — so unlike the colors, none of this can be read directly. Roles were assigned by rendering all six kit files against the actual artwork and comparing letterform details. Two match; four have no usage anywhere in the source file or the release art, and are marked accordingly.

Matched Logotype
Sporty Pro Bold CD

Confirmed against the lockup: the squared-off O with flat sides, the S with horizontal terminals, the equal-length E bars and the slab accent all match glyph for glyph, at both the small "LOS" size and the large wordmark. The reversed Я is a custom modification on top of it.

Use for: the wordmark, and any headline whose content is the band name — there the type and the logo are the same object, so they take the same face. Nothing else — every other headline is Pilat Wide Heavy.

14 DE AGOSTO · REVÉZ RECORDS

Los Revéz publican el segundo adelanto de «Al Derecho… y Los Revéz», grabado en dos sesiones y sin edición por partes.

Bogotá, 14 de agosto — La canción llega hoy a plataformas digitales. Escrita durante los ensayos del año pasado, mantiene el trabajo de guitarras que la banda viene sosteniendo desde su primer sencillo, esta vez sobre una sección rítmica más seca y una letra dirigida a quien alguna vez puso en duda la música como oficio.

La grabación buscó el empuje que el grupo tiene en vivo antes que la limpieza del estudio: tomas completas, la banda tocando junta en la sala, y una mezcla que deja el bajo al frente. El EP completo se publica en noviembre.

Why on Bone

A release is the text-dense case light mode exists for. Ink on Bone runs 11.1:1; the same paragraph reversed out of black is harder to hold across four lines.

Measure

Capped at 34em, about 66 characters. Whitney Condensed fits more per line than a normal-width face, so the em cap is tighter than it looks.

The three sizes

Lead 19px/1.5 at full ink. Body 16px/1.68 at Ink 78%. Meta 12px Khand caps. No indents, no justification — space between paragraphs does the separating.

Dateline

Khand caps run into the first sentence rather than sitting on its own line — it marks the start of the body without costing a paragraph.

Emphasis inside body copy is the open problem: only one Whitney weight was supplied, so a bold run in a paragraph has nothing to become. Request the family's Bold.

Image
Full-bleed, in its own box: the photo area is a flex child at min-height 48vh, so it can never be squeezed out by a long copy block, and object-fit cover frames on that box rather than on the whole hero. Focal point 30% from top, desaturated to 0.85 with a touch of contrast — the photography is already near-monochrome and the treatment keeps it there.
Ink plate
No copy sits on the photograph. The image fades to solid Ink Black across its last 12%, handing off to an opaque plate that carries the whole text block, so contrast is set by the palette and not by the picture: title 15.9:1, eyebrow and CTA 5.8:1, body 11.1:1 — identical over the white-wall studio shot and the near-black cover art. A translucent scrim can't promise that; whichever photograph goes in, these hold.
Title
Sporty Pro at clamp(56px, 13vw, 132px), line-height 0.88, bottom-left, capped at 820px so it never runs the full width of a desktop viewport.
One action
A single gold button. Platform names sit beside it as text, not as competing buttons — the draft's pill row was pulling weight away from the CTA.

Deviations from the draft, deliberate: the accent gold moved from #B8972A to #A89360 as read from the source file, the red date line moved to gold (no red exists in the brand assets), and Anton/Inter were replaced by the real kit. The platform pills became plain text to leave one primary action.

04

Light mode

The inverted pairing, built out: Bone ground, Ink Black for all type and for the lockup, Brass Gold demoted to rules, hairlines and ornament. Nothing that has to be read is gold. This section is set in the mode it documents, so every ratio below is live on this page.

Ink Black
#1B1919 ON #D2CEC3 · 11.1:1
Headlines, body, the lockup, every control label. Passes AAA at any size.
Ink 72%
≈#4E4D49 ON #D2CEC3 · 5.4:1
The one secondary tone — supporting copy and captions. A tint of the brand ink, not a new grey. Clears AA body.
Brass Gold
#A89360 ON #D2CEC3 · 1.9:1
Ornament only — rules, hairlines, the crown. Darkening it doesn't rescue it: the print gold #87764C still only reaches 2.8:1, under even the 3:1 large-text floor. There is no readable gold on Bone.
The CTA, re-treated

The gold button is the one component that can't survive the flip — and not for the reason it looks like. Its label is fine: ink on gold is 5.8:1 either way. What fails is the button's own edge against the page, 1.9:1, well under the 3:1 that WCAG 1.4.11 requires of a control's boundary. The affordance disappears; it reads as a patch of texture, not something you can press. Three replacements, measured:

What flipped
Plate to Bone, all type to ink, and the two gold text items — the eyebrow and the date line — reassigned. The eyebrow keeps its gold as the rule beside it, thickened to 2px so the accent still registers; its words went ink. The date line went Ink 72%.
Photo handoff
Same structure as dark: the image owns its own box and fades to solid Bone across its last 13%, so no copy ever sits on the photograph. Brightness nudged to 1.04 and saturation dropped to 0.7 so the near-monochrome photography meets the paper rather than punching a hole in it.
Which mode when
Dark for anything photographic or in-release — it's the band's default. Light is the covers' own build, so it belongs on print, merch, lyric and credit pages: text-dense surfaces where 11.1:1 ink on paper beats a photograph.
Still unresolved here
Bone itself is sampled from cover art, not read from RVZ 06 — light mode's whole ground rests on an inference. If the band has a specified paper value, it replaces #D2CEC3 and every ratio on this page shifts with it.
